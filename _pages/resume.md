## Professional summary

I have more than 20 years of experience building software, from native-language tools and enterprise applications to cloud-native platforms. My work combines hands-on engineering, solution architecture, technical leadership, and consulting. I focus on agentic systems, distributed architectures, observability, security, and helping teams take solutions into production.

## Experience

### Microsoft

#### Principal Software Engineer, Forward Deployed Engineering

September 2025 to present, Melbourne

My current work focuses on reliable agentic systems, context engineering, agent identity and delegation, observability, and the engineering practices needed to move AI-assisted development beyond prototypes.

Selected public work from this period:

* Presented practical context-engineering techniques for AI coding agents in [Throw Away The Vibes](/2026/06/10/throw-away-the-vibes-context-engineering-ddd-melbourne-2026/), including context isolation, compression, tool selection, and structured Research-Plan-Implement-Review workflows

#### Senior Software Engineer, Commercial Software Engineering

April 2022 to September 2025, Melbourne

I helped customers tackle significant technical challenges with distributed systems and DevOps practices, while enabling their engineering teams to carry solutions into production. The work included multidisciplinary LLM engineering, secure tool use, software supply chains, observability, and Kubernetes deployments in intermittently connected and air-gapped edge environments.

Selected public work from this period:

* Shared production lessons from multidisciplinary teams building LLM-powered customer solutions, covering safety, compliance, misuse, accuracy, and security in [Lessons from the Trenches in an LLM Frontier](/2024/10/30/llm-lessons-api-days-2024/)
* Documented a customer-derived design pattern that keeps identity-scoped parameters outside LLM-generated tool inputs to reduce prompt-injection risk in [LLM Prompt Injection Considerations With Tool Use](/2024/05/03/llm-prompt-injection-considerations-for-tool-use/)
* Co-presented practices used by the team to secure software supply chains for government and defence customers, including SBOMs, OCI artifacts, signing, attestations, and policy enforcement in [Building Trust Brick by Brick](/2024/01/05/secure-supply-chain-api-days-2023/)
* Presented lessons from deploying Kubernetes workloads with GitOps to intermittently connected and air-gapped edge fleets in [EdgeDevOps in the Bush, Air and Underwater](/2023/01/06/edge-devops-apidays-australia-2022/)
* Published [.NET and Python OpenTelemetry samples](https://github.com/dasiths/OpenTelemetryDistributedTracingSample) for trace-context propagation across HTTP, RabbitMQ, and MQTT, including traces, metrics, and logs
* Created the [Breadcrumb Protocol](/2025/04/02/vibe-coding-breadcrumbs/) and its [reference implementation](https://github.com/dasiths/VibeCodingBreadcrumbDemo) to maintain shared context, decisions, and plans between developers and coding agents
